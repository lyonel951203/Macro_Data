# Historical reports

This directory groups completed report families that are kept for lineage and
reproducibility but are not read by the unattended pipeline.

- `archive_parse/` — offline archive parsing runs, review rounds and evidence.
- `nbs/` — completed NBS discovery, backfill and continuity reviews.
- `pboc/` — completed PBOC archive, mirror and pipeline diagnostics, plus the
  preserved pagination page artifact.
- `pit/` — completed PIT construction, availability, continuity and backfill
  work.
- `project_cleanup/` — repository cleanup evidence and removal manifests.
- `quality/` — dated acceptance checks and one-time data-quality audits.
- `wind/` — completed Wind intake batches, revision evidence and gap backfills.

Current daily, weekly, query, audit and status reports remain one level above.
